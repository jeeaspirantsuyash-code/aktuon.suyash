DR. A.P.J. ABDUL KALAM TECHNICAL UNIVERSITY, LUCKNOW

B.TECH (SEM V) THEORY EXAMINATION 2023-24

DATABASE MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS [KCS501]

Time: 3 Hours Total Marks: 100

Note: Attempt all Sections. If you require any missing data; choose suitably.

SECTION A (10 × 2 = 20 Marks)

  1. Explain the difference between Physical and Logical Data Independence.
  2. What is the primary role of a storage manager component in DBMS architecture?
  3. Define Functional Dependency with an example.
  4. Why is 3NF preferred over BCNF in practical industry schemas?
  5. Define Strict Two-Phase Locking (2PL) protocol.

SECTION B (3 × 10 = 30 Marks)

  1. Construct an E-R Diagram for a University Examination System highlighting weak entities.
  2. Consider schema R(A, B, C, D, E) with FDs {A->BC, CD->E, B->D, E->A}. Find all candidate keys and normal form.
  3. Describe Shadow Paging recovery technique and compare it with Log-based Immediate update.
